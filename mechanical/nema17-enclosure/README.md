# NEMA17 controller enclosure — v4 fit-test draft

Native CAD source and inputs for the enclosure previously published in the
[assembly viewer](https://tscircuit-nema17-enclosure.anassoftwaredev.chatgpt.site/).
The four printable parts retain the original geometry. This import does not
change the board, motor, screw length, or enclosure dimensions.

The downloads are served from `dist/assets/nema17-enclosure/` and listed on
[`assets.html`](../../dist/assets.html#enclosure-heading). `catalog.json`
records the revision, part names and SHA-256 hashes of all eight CAD downloads.

| Part | Height | Exterior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain top (`top-laser`) | 6.8 mm | Flat; no sticker pocket or wordmark recess |
| Sticker top (`top-sticker`) | 6.8 mm | 38.7 × 19.65 × 0.3 mm label pocket |
| Wordmark top (`top-recessed`) | 6.8 mm | 0.3 mm recessed tscircuit lettering |
| Bottom housing (`bottom`) | 3.4 mm | Continuous lower JST wall; replaces the old spacer |

All parts have a nominal 42 × 42 mm footprint, 31 × 31 mm mounting pattern,
and Ø3.6 mm printed-part shaft bores. Top counterbores are Ø6.6 mm with a
Ø7 mm entry chamfer. The assembled rear-plane-to-top height is 11.8 mm,
including the 1.6 mm PCB. The original CAD coordinates are retained; a slicer
should place each imported part on the bed rather than treating assembly Z as
a required print height.

## First fit print

Choose the **plain top** for a flat exterior without the sticker indentation.
Try printing exterior face down and inspect the slicer's overhangs and first
layer. The bolt counterbores and connector openings remain. This is not a
claim that every printer/material will print it without any supports. Keep
supports away from mating surfaces where possible, and remove them fully
before checking fit. The sticker top's pocket prevents the same fully flat
bed contact; choose its orientation and supports deliberately.

The design was checked against the published **RP2040 motor-controller PCB
v1.0.57**, not every NEMA17 board supported by this firmware repository. The
motor is a reconstructed 42 × 42 × 34 mm external reference; its actual rear
geometry and bolt engagement are unconfirmed. Physically check the board
revision, motor, bolts, USB cable overmolds and JST mating cable. The 40 mm
bolts in the external assembly viewer are references, not a selected screw
length for this stack. See `dist/assets/nema17-enclosure/fit-notes.txt`.

## Rebuild CAD

The native OpenCascade/CadQuery tool is preserved as Python; new web UI source
is TypeScript under `src/`. Use an isolated Python 3.11/3.12 environment:

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv-enclosure
.venv-enclosure/bin/pip install -r mechanical/nema17-enclosure/requirements.txt
.venv-enclosure/bin/python mechanical/nema17-enclosure/build-cad.py
.venv-enclosure/bin/python mechanical/nema17-enclosure/validate-clearance.py
```

The builder writes native STEP and STL to its ignored `models/` folder and
validates component-envelope clearance. The validator checks all four parts,
bores, screw pockets and material around the pockets. To publish a deliberate
new revision, copy the exported `cover-laser-mark-draft`,
`cover-sticker-pocket-draft`, `cover-recessed-logo-draft` and
`insulating-base-draft` files into the corresponding `top-laser`, `top-sticker`,
`top-recessed` and `bottom` download paths, then update the catalog hashes and
revision. Motor-reference STEP files emitted by the builder are illustrative
and are not distributed as manufacturer CAD.

## Label source

The copied PDF/SVG and preview are generated by
[`tscircuit/product-packaging`](https://github.com/tscircuit/product-packaging/pull/8),
`products/nema17/enclosure-label`. `label-source-v6.json` records the precise
source commit and output hashes. The text is **NEMA17 USB-PD** / **Stepper
driver & controller**, with the URL below. Edit and regenerate the label there;
do not create a second generator here. Print its OL1067 60-up US Letter sheet
at 100% / Actual Size, and check alignment on plain paper first.
