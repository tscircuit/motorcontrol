REVISION v4 — MORE BOLT CLEARANCE
Head pocket: Ø6.6 mm (previously Ø6.0 mm), 3.2 mm deep.
Nominal Ø5.5 mm head radial clearance: 0.55 mm per side (previously 0.25 mm).
Top and bottom printed-part shaft bores: Ø3.6 mm (previously Ø3.4 mm).
Nominal Ø3.0 mm shaft radial clearance: 0.30 mm per side.
Entry chamfer: Ø7.0 mm maximum, 0.2 mm deep.
Post outside diameter: Ø8.4 mm (previously Ø7.8 mm), leaving 0.9 mm
nominal wall beside the counterbore. Component reliefs remain board-specific.
Motor and PCB holes are source/reference geometry and have not been enlarged.
The 31 x 31 mm bolt pattern, 8.6 mm head-seat Z and 11.8 mm stack are unchanged.
CAD checks: all three lids and bottom housing are valid single solids;
all 16 printed-part bore positions clear; all 12 lid pockets clear;
post-wall probes and conservative component-clearance checks pass.
This nominal allowance is a prototype starting point, not a physical print test.
Print one corner or one case and check the actual bolts before a batch.
Viewer default 40 mm under-head length is a reference, not an approved motor fit.
